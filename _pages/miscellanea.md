Outside of research, here are a few things about me.

🏀 Hobbies
======
------

I enjoy playing **basketball** and **swimming**, and I love **music**, especially Cantonese songs and piano music. I am a big fan of **Hins Cheung** (张敬轩) and **Stefanie Sun** (孙燕姿). I also practice **Chinese calligraphy** (Gr. 10).

🎮 Gaming
======
------

I am a **League of Legends** player. I mainly play top lane (Fiora, Camille) and mid lane (Akali, Diana), as well as hyper-carry ADCs such as Aphelios and Zeri.

📚 Reading
======
------
